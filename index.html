Training guidance

Start with manageable weights and controlled technique. A sensible starting point is 2 working sets per exercise. Leave some reps in reserve rather than lifting to exhaustion. Rest 60–120 seconds, longer if needed.

Safety first

This is a planning aid, not a medical assessment. Don't train through pain or use exercise swaps to work around a possible injury. For sharp, worsening or unusual pain, swelling, weakness or an injury, stop and speak to a parent/guardian and an appropriate health professional. If unsure, rest.
